# Research corpora

Research corpora preserve source identity and evidence separately from promoted domain knowledge.

## Active / preserved corpora

- `railly-skills-2026-09-22/` — exact Railly/skills snapshot used by EM3RC0D Foundry.
- `whatsapp-links-2025-2026/` — provenance-preserving web-source corpus.
- `social-media-scraping-apis-2026-09-23/` — commercial/API discovery corpus.
- `em3rc0d-repositories-2026-10-01/` — pinned repository-estate corpus spanning first-party systems and explicitly classified upstream forks.
- [`inditextech-2026-10-08/`](./inditextech-2026-10-08/) — pinned 40-repository external source atlas plus candidate patterns and explicit non-adoption gates; MK0/quarry only.

## Rule

A corpus answers **what source/evidence was captured**. It does not answer **what EM3RC0D currently believes or should build**. Promotion remains domain-specific and gate-driven.
