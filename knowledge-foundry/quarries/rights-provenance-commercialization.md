# Quarry — Rights, Provenance and Commercialization

Source: `../mining-site/S-005-rights-provenance.md`  
Status: **NON-CANONICAL / MK0 input**

## Gap found

The public `personal_knowledge` repository has no root LICENSE file.

That is not automatically an error: it may be intentional when preserving commercial control. But the posture must be explicit before knowledge products are distributed.

## Candidate rules

### RP-01 Productization requires a rights graph, not just citations

Track ownership/licensing for:

- source text;
- code;
- diagrams;
- screenshots;
- photos;
- video/audio;
- fonts/assets;
- exercises/fixtures;
- generated material.

### RP-02 Product license and dependency license are independent

A course-level license cannot grant rights we do not own.

### RP-03 Unknown rights fail closed for reproduced material

When commercial/adaptation rights are unknown, keep the source reference-only or obtain permission.

### RP-04 Do not apply a repo-wide license casually

The repository contains our material plus preserved third-party corpora with their own licenses. A blanket license could create ambiguity.

### RP-05 Provenance should survive compilation

Pin the source entity/revision and the compile/review activity. W3C PROV vocabulary is a useful conceptual reference.

## Decision still required

Whether future original course content is:

- all-rights-reserved;
- CC licensed;
- dual licensed;
- selectively open;

is a business/legal decision and remains OPEN.
