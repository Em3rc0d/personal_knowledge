# Quarry — Assessment Validity and Fairness

Source: `../mining-site/S-003-assessment-quality.md`  
Status: **NON-CANONICAL / MK0 input**

## Candidate rules

### AS-01 Interpretation before score

Define what an assessment result is intended to mean before choosing the question/task.

### AS-02 Claim strength proportional to validation strength

Low-stakes practice can use lightweight checks.

Credential/mastery claims require stronger validity, reliability and fairness evidence.

### AS-03 Direct evidence for performance outcomes

If an outcome says `design`, `implement`, `diagnose` or `defend`, an MCQ alone is usually insufficient evidence for that outcome.

### AS-04 Fairness is not optional polish

Construct-irrelevant barriers reduce the defensibility of the interpretation.

### AS-05 Feedback closes the loop

Assessment evidence should inform learner/instructional next steps where the assessment is formative.

## Anti-patterns

- course completion percentage used as mastery;
- satisfaction survey used as learning proof;
- one grader/model used as unquestioned ground truth;
- scoring rubric changed after seeing desired outcomes;
- inaccessible assessment treated as learner deficiency.
