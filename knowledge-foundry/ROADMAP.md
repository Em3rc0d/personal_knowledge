# Knowledge Foundry — Roadmap

Current stage: **MK0 — Mine & Frame**

The roadmap answers: **what must be true before we are allowed to compile and sell knowledge as an educational product?**

## MK0 — Mine & Frame

### Workstream A — Identity and boundaries

- define Knowledge Foundry responsibility;
- prevent duplication of technical canon;
- define interaction with `em3rc0d-foundry/`;
- define output/product ownership;
- define the no-direct-source-to-course rule.

Exit: G0 passes.

### Workstream B — Source and provenance discipline

- source registry;
- pinned source identity when possible;
- provenance class;
- license status;
- allowed transformation/use boundary;
- distinction between inspiration, evidence and reusable material.

Exit: G1 passes.

### Workstream C — Knowledge compiler

Define the minimum compiler stages:

```text
eligible knowledge
→ candidate
→ concept graph
→ learning outcomes
→ lesson/lab/assessment specs
→ provenance manifest
→ pilot
→ promotion
```

Avoid building a runtime or platform in MK0.

Exit: G2 passes.

### Workstream D — Educational product contract

Define the minimum durable artifacts for one product:

- product brief;
- audience/prerequisite contract;
- concept graph;
- curriculum;
- lesson specs;
- lab specs;
- assessment spec;
- source/license manifest;
- pilot receipt;
- release receipt.

Exit: G3 passes.

### Workstream E — Pilot and evidence

Define how we falsify a product before release:

- can target learners complete the tasks?
- do assessments test transfer, not recall only?
- where do learners get stuck?
- which outcomes were not demonstrated?
- what changes after feedback?
- what evidence is sufficient for promotion?

Exit: G4 passes.

## MK0 closure

MK0 closes only when G0–G4 pass and remaining unknowns are either resolved or explicitly routed to MK1.

Closure output:

`mk/MK0/CLOSURE.md`

## MK1 — Normalize & Classify (future)

MK1 should normalize reusable educational primitives rather than write a full catalog of courses.

Candidate surfaces:

- learning outcome taxonomy;
- prerequisite/dependency model;
- lesson archetypes;
- lab archetypes;
- assessment types;
- misconception patterns;
- evidence classes;
- product lifecycle states;
- update/versioning semantics.

MK1 does **not** activate until MK0 closure.

## First dogfood

Candidate domains may include `agent-engineering`, `web-design`, `ux-laws` or another sufficiently mature area.

Selection is evidence-driven. No candidate is pre-approved merely because it seems marketable.
