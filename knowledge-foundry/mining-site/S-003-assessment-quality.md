# S-003 — Assessment Quality and Validity

Observed: 2026-10-07  
Status: **INSPECTED**  
Role: assessment validity, reliability, fairness and use boundaries.

## Primary standards

### Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing

Joint work of AERA, APA and NCME. The 2014 edition is available open access and remains the published standard while a revision process is underway.

Identity:
- https://www.testingstandards.net/
- https://www.testingstandards.net/open-access-files.html

Key boundary adopted for research:

> validity concerns whether accumulated evidence and theory support a specific interpretation of scores for a given use.

This means the same score may need different evidence when used for different decisions.

### NCME Classroom Assessment Standards

Identity:
https://ncme.org/involvement/committees/classroom-assessment/classroom-assessment-standards/

Relevant observed dimensions include:

- appropriate analysis of learning evidence;
- timely/useful feedback;
- instructional follow-up;
- sufficient evidence for reporting;
- cultural/linguistic responsiveness;
- unbiased/fair assessment;
- reliability and validity;
- reflection/revision.

## Use boundary

Knowledge Foundry is not building a psychometric testing organization in MK0.

These standards are used to prevent weak inferences such as:

```text
quiz score → mastery
completion → competence
satisfaction → learning
```

The strength of validation required must remain proportional to the consequence of the claim.
