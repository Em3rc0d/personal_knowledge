# S-004 — Accessibility and Inclusive Learning Design

Observed: 2026-10-07  
Status: **INSPECTED**

## W3C WCAG 2.2

Identity: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

WCAG 2.2 is a W3C Recommendation. W3C advises use of WCAG 2.2 to maximize future applicability of accessibility work.

Role in Knowledge Foundry:

- technical accessibility/conformance baseline for web content;
- not proof of pedagogical effectiveness.

## CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0

Identity: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/

CAST published UDL Guidelines 3.0 in 2024. The framework organizes considerations across engagement, representation, and action/expression and is intended to help reduce barriers in learning environments.

Role in Knowledge Foundry:

- inclusive-learning design lens;
- not a substitute for WCAG conformance;
- not treated as a numeric certification score.
