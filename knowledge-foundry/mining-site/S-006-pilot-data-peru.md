## Observed current boundary

The new Regulation entered into force on 31 March 2025. Official ANPD guidance states that holders of personal-data banks have registration obligations, and official MINJUSDH material highlights preventive impact-assessment/security mechanisms.

## Knowledge Foundry consequence

A future learner pilot may create personal-data processing through:

- identity/contact data;
- assessment results;
- behavioral telemetry;
- feedback;
- recordings.

Therefore pilot design must establish a data boundary before collection.

No jurisdiction-specific compliance conclusion is promoted from this receipt. Applicability must be checked against the actual product/data flow at launch.
