# EM3RC0D Foundry — Mining Sources

A source is evidence, not canon.

## Provenance vocabulary

- OFFICIAL — first-party documentation/source from the system owner.
- OBSERVED — artifact, code, metadata or behavior directly inspected.
- INFERRED — conclusion derived from explicit observations.
- INSPIRED — adapted mechanism, not claimed as the source’s own rule.
- GENERATED — EM3RC0D proposal awaiting evidence.
