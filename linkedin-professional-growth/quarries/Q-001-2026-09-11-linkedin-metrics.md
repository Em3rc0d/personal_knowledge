3. SQL content explains most of the observed weekly impressions.
4. Profile views accelerated sharply, but profile-click-through remains small relative to reach.
5. Search appearances were flat while feed distribution accelerated.
6. Conversation and shares are small relative to saves/reactions.
7. Current growth appears content-led rather than search-led.

## Interpretation boundary

The data does **not** by itself prove:

- that every SQL topic will reproduce the same reach;
- that 4 posts/day is globally optimal;
- that LinkedIn directly converted this reach into paid opportunities;
- that Buenos Aires / LATAM distribution will persist;
- that a specific CTA caused comments or saves.

Those remain hypotheses for longitudinal validation.
