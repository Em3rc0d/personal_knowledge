### Important boundary

The platform metrics establish **what happened on the platform**, not why it happened.

Claims such as:

- “SQL found product-market fit of content”,
- “students amplify distribution toward recruiters”,
- “four posts/day should remain active”,
- “proof-of-work will increase hiring opportunities”,

are `INFERRED` or `GENERATED` hypotheses unless validated longitudinally.
