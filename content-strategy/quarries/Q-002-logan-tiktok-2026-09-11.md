## 6. Aggressive growth — what it means here

`GENERATED`

Aggressive means:

- direct symptom framing;
- strong first-frame tension;
- consequence framing when mechanically defensible;
- useful curiosity gaps that are resolved inside the post;
- “before replacing X, check Y” logic when evidence supports it;
- sharper visual hierarchy;
- explicit reasons to comment, save, share or visit the profile.

Aggressive does **not** mean:

- fabricated danger;
- fake certainty from a single symptom;
- unrelated shock wording;
- generic “NO VAS A CREER ESTO” packaging with no technical payoff;
- forcing comments to reveal information promised by the hook.

The operating rule is:

```text
attention gained <= value actually delivered
```

## 7. Symptom-first content architecture

Default pattern:

```text
SYMPTOM
  → INTRIGUE
  → PLAUSIBLE CAUSES
  → HOW TO DIFFERENTIATE
  → NEXT STEP
  → ONE CTA
```

Prefer:

> “¿Tu carro pierde fuerza cuando aceleras?”

rather than:

> “Sensor MAF: funcionamiento”.

The component is often the answer, not the hook.

## 9. No-repeat contract

Before approving a post, compare it against recent output on:

```text
topic
symptom
diagnosis
hook semantic pattern
creative structure
visual metaphor
carousel progression
punchline
CTA structure
```

Blocked by default:

- same diagnosis with a different headline;
- same symptom with cosmetic wording changes;
- same meme premise;
- same carousel progression repeatedly;
- disguised Part 2 without an explicit continuation reason.

Allowed when explicitly labeled:

- controlled variation;
- continuation / Part 2;
- correction;
- new evidence;
- deeper treatment requested by the audience.

## 10. Visual quality rule

Four posts/day does not authorize four mediocre assets.

Minimum gate:

- mobile-first first frame;
- readable text without zoom;
- one dominant idea per slide;
- technical imagery that clarifies rather than decorates;
- deliberate hierarchy;
- consistent identity without repetitive composition;
- each requested carousel slide exported as a separately usable asset.

## 11. CTA-by-objective

One primary job per CTA.

```text
comments  → ask for a concrete symptom / condition / experience
saves     → make the post reference-worthy
shares    → make forwarding useful or identity-relevant
follows   → promise a coherent future stream
profile   → create a reason to inspect the account, not a vague “link in bio” habit
```

Avoid mechanically ending every post with “follow me”.

## 14. Working classification model

`GENERATED / provisional`

### GREEN

Beats the rolling median in at least two objective-relevant metrics or produces an unusually strong downstream signal.

Action: extract the winning mechanism and test up to 3 materially different variations within about 72h.

### YELLOW

Topic appears useful but packaging is weak or mixed.

Action: change hook, format, CTA or visual treatment and retry with a documented reason.

### RED

Three materially distinct attempts fail against the intended objective.

Action: archive the angle and preserve the evidence rather than recycling it silently.

Thresholds remain provisional until enough longitudinal data exists.
