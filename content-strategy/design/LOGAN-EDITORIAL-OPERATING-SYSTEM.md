## 1. Objective

Turn Logan into a repeatable **organic audience-acquisition system** for automotive content.

Immediate goal:

```text
MORE RELEVANT PEOPLE
→ STOP
→ CONSUME
→ SHARE / RESPOND
→ PROFILE
→ FOLLOW
```

The account is not currently optimized for product conversion. The public job is audience growth, usefulness and trust.

## 3. Editorial thesis

Write to the **driver and the decision**, not to the component.

Prefer:

> “¿Tu carro hace esto cuando frenas?”

over:

> “Funcionamiento del componente X”.

The strongest reusable mechanism is:

```text
recognizable symptom / belief / habit
→ tension or contradiction
→ concise technical reasoning
→ useful decision
```

Reuse the mechanism. Do not repost the topic.

## 4. Hard no-repeat contract

Before approving any idea, compare it against the historical ledger and recent output on:

```text
topic
symptom
diagnosis
belief / myth
decision
hook semantic pattern
creative structure
visual metaphor
carousel progression
punchline
CTA structure
```

A topic already published is `PUBLISHED` and blocked by default.

A close semantic family is `COOLDOWN` when it risks feeling like the same value proposition.

Only genuinely unused territory is `NEW`.

```text
PUBLISHED  → do not repost
COOLDOWN   → avoid unless materially different and justified
NEW        → eligible
```

A new headline does not make an old topic new.

Explicit continuation / correction / new evidence is allowed only when intentionally requested or documented.

## 6. Four-slot growth system

Current default while quality survives:

### SLOT A — SEARCH

Goal: capture existing intent.

Input:

```text
real query / symptom language
```

Output:

```text
query-aligned cover
+ natural explanation
+ search-aligned caption
```

### SLOT B — HERO

Goal: attempt to break the account’s ordinary distribution cluster.

Properties:

- broad recognizable situation;
- strongest hook of the day;
- lowest unnecessary cognitive load;
- immediate visual comprehension;
- one memorable payoff.

### SLOT C — SHARE ENGINE

Goal: make forwarding useful or socially recognizable.

Design for:

- “this is exactly what happened to me”;
- “someone I know does this”;
- “send this before they spend money”;
- concise reference value.

Do not rely on “share this” as the reason to share.

### SLOT D — LAB

Goal: learn.

Test one meaningful variable:

- hook family;
- format;
- photo-real vs infographic;
- POV;
- A/B decision;
- short case;
- tuning/performance subtopic;
- meme mechanic;
- motion/audio when the phenomenon benefits from it.

A Lab post must be labeled `EXPLORATORY` in the ledger.

## 7. Hook architecture

The first frame is a scarce resource.

Default target:

```text
one idea
~4–9 words when practical
understandable in about one second
directly about the viewer / vehicle / decision
```

### Family A — Risk

> “SI TU CARRO HACE ESTO, NO LO IGNORES.”

Use only when risk is real and explained.

### Family B — Money

> “ANTES DE PAGAR POR ESTO, REVISA ESTO.”

No invented prices or savings.

### Family C — Diagnostic uncertainty

> “PARECE X. NO SIEMPRE ES X.”

### Family D — Decision conflict

> “EL TALLER DICE X. ¿QUÉ REVISARÍAS PRIMERO?”

### Family E — Misread signal

> “ESTE NÚMERO NO SIGNIFICA LO QUE CREES.”

Only on a new topic.

### Family F — Cost of guessing

> “CAMBIAR PIEZAS A CIEGAS EMPIEZA ASÍ.”

Use only when the body gives a concrete verification path.

## 8. Aggressiveness boundary

Aggressive:

```text
specific
fast
high-stakes when justified
recognizable
contrasting
visually dominant
technically useful
```

Not aggressive:

```text
fake danger
fake certainty
fabricated repair bill
rage bait
unsupported diagnosis
generic “NO VAS A CREER”
answer withheld to force comments
```

Integrity rule:

```text
attention gained <= value delivered
```

## 9. Retention architecture

### Carousel

Default:

```text
Slide 1  hook / unresolved question
Slide 2  first useful clue
Slide 3  distinction / why obvious answer may be wrong
Slide 4  payoff / what to verify
Slide 5  only if needed: concise decision or interaction trigger
```

Do not add slides for volume.

Each slide must change the informational state.

### Video

Default:

```text
0–1s    recognizable problem
1–3s    tension / contradiction
3–8s    evidence or clue
8–15s   explanation
final   payoff / decision / natural conversation trigger
```

Duration is determined by the idea, not a fixed magic number.

### Single image

Use when one visual can carry the complete value proposition immediately.

## 10. Visual system

Target:

> **premium + TikTok-native**

Not:

> corporate infographic deck.

Rules:

- 9:16;
- mobile-size readability;
- one dominant message;
- real parts / dashboards / situations when recognition benefits;
- diagrams only when they clarify;
- photo + annotation is preferred over decorative complexity when faster;
- no white bands, blurred assets or low-resolution exports;
- each carousel slide exported separately;
- no repeated layout across the entire batch;
- visual identity stays coherent without becoming template fatigue.

## 11. Search operating rule

Every Search post starts with a target query.

```text
TARGET_QUERY
COVER
BODY_TERMS
CAPTION_OPEN
TOPIC_TAGS
```

Query alignment should be natural across:

- cover text;
- spoken/visible wording;
- caption opening;
- topic-specific hashtags.

Do not keyword-stuff.

Do not depend on `#fyp` as a discovery strategy.

## 12. Comments strategy

Current generic CTA behavior has not produced meaningful comments.

Blocked as default endings:

- “¿qué opinas?”;
- “guerra civil en comentarios”;
- “etiqueta a tu amigo”;
- arbitrary “comenta X”.

Prefer **decision tension**:

> “¿Revisarías A o B primero?”

> “¿Te pasa en frío o con el motor caliente?”

> “El taller dice A. El síntoma también encaja con B. ¿Qué dato pedirías?”

The question must be answerable from experience or reasoning.

## 13. Share strategy

Make the post itself worth sending.

Best share candidates tend to contain:

- a preventable mistake;
- a money-saving verification;
- a recognizable habit;
- a clear safety distinction;
- a surprising but defensible correction;
- a compact checklist.

Do not confuse a share CTA with shareability.
