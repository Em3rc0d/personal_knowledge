# MK0 — Mine & Frame

## Objective

Build a trustworthy evidence base for the UX Laws domain before generating prescriptive design rules.

## Inputs

- Laws of UX Spanish and English catalogs.
- Original/primary references linked or attributable to each principle.
- Modern HCI/psychology literature where the popular formulation may be simplified.
- Accessibility, ethics and security considerations.
- Real product/interface observations when explicitly tagged `OBSERVED`.

## Workstreams

```text
MINING-SITE
  source discovery + provenance
      ↓
QUARRIES
  extracted law dossiers
      ↓
BRAINSTORMING
  relationships / hypotheses / questions
      ↓
DESIGN + ARCHITECTURE
  not normative yet; only candidate structures
      ↓
PLAN
  MK1 closure plan
```

`build/` and `test/` in MK0 concern **knowledge tooling/contracts**, not production UX implementation.

## Non-goals

MK0 MUST NOT:

- declare universal numeric UI limits from simplified psychology claims;
- claim our taxonomy is official Laws of UX taxonomy;
- turn heuristics into mandatory rules without applicability boundaries;
- certify design-system behavior;
- present copied source prose as original knowledge;
- hide uncertainty or disagreement.

## Deliverables

1. 30 individual law dossiers.
2. EN↔ES canonical name map.
3. origin/reference map.
4. caveat and misuse registry.
5. relationship/conflict graph.
6. provenance and confidence schema.
7. copyright/source-use boundary.
8. MK0 review report.

## Exit condition

MK0 closes only when every required artifact exists and the review demonstrates that evidence, inference and generated guidance are distinguishable.
