## Source-level summaries

The following are concise paraphrases of the seed source and are tagged `OFFICIAL / PARAPHRASED`.

- `UXL-001`: people focus only on a subset of environmental stimuli, usually those relevant to current goals.
- `UXL-002`: interfaces require finite mental resources to understand and operate.
- `UXL-003`: aesthetically pleasing designs are often perceived as easier to use.
- `UXL-004`: first and last items in a sequence tend to be remembered better.
- `UXL-005`: motivation to approach a goal tends to rise as the goal gets closer.
- `UXL-006`: a distinctive item among similar items is more likely to stand out and be remembered.
- `UXL-007`: incomplete/interrupted tasks tend to remain more cognitively salient than completed tasks.
- `UXL-008`: flow describes deep, energized immersion in an activity.
- `UXL-009`: grouping information into meaningful units can improve processing.
- `UXL-010`: working memory temporarily holds/manipulates task-relevant information.
- `UXL-011`: among equally predictive explanations, prefer the one requiring fewer assumptions.
- `UXL-012`: visually connected elements are perceived as more related.
- `UXL-013`: target acquisition time depends on target distance and size.
- `UXL-014`: decision time tends to increase with the number and complexity of choices.
- `UXL-015`: users bring interaction expectations learned from other products.
- `UXL-016`: visually similar elements tend to be perceived as related.
- `UXL-017`: the seed source presents the classic `7 ± 2` working-memory formulation; this requires modern nuance before operationalization.
- `UXL-018`: tasks tend to expand to fill the time available.
- `UXL-019`: classic robustness principle: tolerate what is received while being strict in what is produced; modern security caveats must be researched.
- `UXL-020`: spatially close objects tend to be perceived as a group.
- `UXL-021`: ambiguous/complex forms tend to be interpreted in the simplest available way.
- `UXL-022`: elements inside a clearly bounded common area tend to be perceived as grouped.
- `UXL-023`: some system complexity is irreducible and must be borne somewhere in the system/user relationship.
- `UXL-024`: users rely on compressed internal beliefs about how systems work.
- `UXL-025`: users often begin using products instead of reading manuals first.
- `UXL-026`: effects are often distributed unevenly across causes; the `80/20` wording is heuristic, not a universal guarantee.
- `UXL-027`: remembered experience is disproportionately influenced by peak moments and the ending.
- `UXL-028`: systematic judgment shortcuts/errors influence perception and decisions.
- `UXL-029`: too many alternatives can overwhelm users and impair choice.
- `UXL-030`: the seed source highlights interaction around `<400 ms` as a threshold for perceived productive dialogue; implementation meaning requires context.
