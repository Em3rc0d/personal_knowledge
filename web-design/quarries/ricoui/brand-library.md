# RICOUI Quarry — Brand Library

Source: `SRC-RICOUI-BRANDS` plus RICOUI attribution documentation.

## Q-RICOUI-BRAND-001 — Reference corpus

**Classification:** `OFFICIAL + OBSERVED`

RICOUI exposes read-only Brand references that can be copied into a user's own draft for analysis and further work.

The corpus includes semantic descriptions such as visual atmosphere, color roles, typography, component language and category/tags rather than functioning only as a screenshot board.

## Q-RICOUI-BRAND-002 — Attribution boundary

**Classification:** `OFFICIAL`

RICOUI explicitly states that built-in Brand-reference material is derived from getdesign.md and VoltAgent/awesome-design-md, then secondarily parsed for its workflow. It also states that the material is for learning/analysis and that not every derived value is claimed to be confirmed by the original brand.

Therefore:

```text
RICOUI brand value
      ≠
official brand guideline
```

unless independently confirmed by a first-party source.

## Q-RICOUI-BRAND-003 — Retrieval potential

**Classification:** `INFERRED`

A corpus tagged by characteristics such as dark/light, editorial, minimal, developer-focused or luxury can be used as a retrieval layer:

```text
product goals
  → desired visual attributes
  → retrieve relevant references
  → compare patterns
  → synthesize original direction
```

The goal is **not** to reproduce a brand. The goal is to narrow and inform the design search space.

## Q-RICOUI-BRAND-004 — Time sensitivity

**Classification:** `GENERATED rule candidate`

Visual observations must record retrieval/observation time when they may change. Public websites evolve; a mined design profile is a snapshot, not eternal truth.
