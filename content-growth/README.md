# Content Growth

Knowledge domain for **metrics-driven content growth, editorial experimentation and distribution-to-conversion systems**.

The objective is not to collect generic social-media advice. This domain converts real account behavior, platform analytics and repeated publishing experiments into **traceable, reusable and testable operating rules**.

## Mission

Turn content performance into a closed learning loop:

```text
platform evidence
  → observation
  → hypothesis
  → editorial decision
  → content experiment
  → measurement
  → promotion / rewrite / kill
  → reusable rule
```

The first active case is **Logan / `mi.logan.pe`**, an automotive TikTok account being optimized for reach, interaction, profile conversion and follower growth.

## Evolution model

```text
MK0  Mine & frame real metrics, content history and hypotheses
 ↓
MK1  Normalize taxonomy, metrics and experiment units
 ↓
MK2  Operationalize editorial rules, templates and gates
 ↓
MK3  Integrate multi-channel / multi-account strategy
 ↓
MK4  Automate collection, backlog generation and publishing support
 ↓
MK5+ Certify predictive rules and refine from longitudinal evidence
```

MK numbers represent knowledge maturity, not arbitrary releases.

## Provenance

- `OBSERVED`: directly visible in analytics, post performance or platform behavior.
- `INFERRED`: interpretation derived from observations.
- `GENERATED`: strategy, rule, framework or artifact synthesized from evidence.
- `OFFICIAL`: platform documentation or authoritative source.
- `INSPIRED`: useful direction influenced by an external source without being claimed as fact.

No metric-driven rule should silently move from correlation to causation.

## Initial operating thesis — Logan

Current evidence supports this working model:

```text
SYMPTOM / PROBLEM
      ↓
STRONG HOOK
      ↓
TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
      ↓
USEFUL DIAGNOSIS / NEXT STEP
      ↓
SPECIFIC CTA
      ↓
VIEW → INTERACTION → PROFILE → FOLLOW
```

The account should not behave as a generic automotive encyclopedia. Its strongest current positioning is:

> **Explain what might be happening with your car before you replace parts blindly.**

This is an `INFERRED / GENERATED` positioning derived from current Logan account evidence, not a permanent truth. It must continue to be tested.

## Core rules already promoted

1. **Distribution is not the same as conversion.** High `For You` exposure can coexist with weak comments, shares, profile visits and follows.
2. **Lead with the symptom, not the component.** Prefer “¿Tu carro hace X?” over “Cómo funciona el sensor X”.
3. **Aggressive does not mean deceptive.** Hooks may maximize tension, specificity and curiosity, but the body must fully deliver the promise.
4. **Every post is an experiment.** Topic, hook, format, complexity, slide count and publishing time are variables.
5. **Use four daily posts as rapid learning capacity while quality remains controlled.** Cadence exists to accelerate evidence collection, not to justify filler.
6. **Do not repeat recent topics, hooks, diagnoses or creative structures unless explicitly testing a documented variation.**
7. **Prefer diagnostic/problem-solving content over generic lifestyle content while the evidence continues to favor it.**
8. **Hashtags should classify the content semantically.** Generic tags such as `#fyp` or unrelated tags are not a substitute for topic clarity.
9. **CTA must match the objective.** Save, comment, follow, profile visit or later conversion are different jobs.
10. **Search queries are product research.** Questions people type into TikTok should feed the editorial backlog.

## Measurement windows

Default evaluation windows:

```text
T+2h   early distribution / hook signal
T+24h  primary post performance
T+7d   stabilized comparison / long-tail signal
```

Track, when available:

- views;
- average watch time;
- completion rate;
- likes;
- shares;
- saves;
- comments;
- profile visits;
- follows attributed or temporally associated;
- search traffic / queries;
- clicks, registrations or sales when a downstream offer exists.

Raw views alone are insufficient.

## Decision model

A working experiment can be classified using account-relative baselines:

- **GREEN** — beats the rolling median in at least two target metrics: produce up to 3 documented variations within ~72h.
- **YELLOW** — useful topic but weak packaging: rewrite opening, visual format or CTA before abandoning it.
- **RED** — after 3 materially distinct attempts fail against the chosen objective: archive the angle and preserve the evidence.

The exact thresholds are provisional until MK1 normalizes sufficient longitudinal data.

## Scope boundaries

This domain stores the reusable growth system. Account-specific evidence stays explicitly labeled by account and date so Logan observations do not silently become universal rules for LinkedIn, technical TikTok, Content Seller or future Prompt Machine channels.
