## Primary diagnosis of the account

`INFERRED / confidence: medium-high`

TikTok is already giving the account non-trivial recommendation distribution. Therefore the immediate optimization problem is not simply:

> “How do we get any reach?”

The higher-value question is:

```text
VIEW
  ↓
INTERACTION
  ↓
PROFILE VISIT
  ↓
FOLLOW
```

The reviewed numbers suggest leakage after the initial view: relatively few comments, shares and profile visits compared with total impressions.

### Working bottleneck

**Distribution → conversion.**

This remains a hypothesis to validate longitudinally.
