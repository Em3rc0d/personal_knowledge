# Content Growth — STATUS

## Current maturity

**MK0 — Mine & Frame: ACTIVE / baseline captured**

The Logan case has enough evidence to establish a first canonical operating thesis, but not enough longitudinal data to claim stable causal rules.

## Completed

- Captured Logan TikTok baseline metrics for the 7-day window reviewed on 2026-09-11.
- Separated direct observations from inferred strategy.
- Identified the current bottleneck as **distribution-to-conversion**, not raw reach alone.
- Promoted symptom-first diagnostic content as the primary editorial hypothesis.
- Preserved four-post daily cadence as an experimental throughput rule, conditional on quality.
- Added no-repeat discipline for topics, hooks, diagnoses and creative structures.
- Added semantic hashtag guidance and CTA-by-objective logic.
- Added `T+2h / T+24h / T+7d` measurement windows.
- Added GREEN / YELLOW / RED experiment gates.
- Added search-query mining as an editorial backlog source.

## In progress

- Accumulate enough post-level observations to compute stable rolling medians by format and objective.
- Separate performance by format: single visual, carousel, meme, short video.
- Measure conversion rates beyond likes: shares, comments, profile visits and follows.
- Build a topic/angle ledger that prevents accidental repetition.
- Test whether symptom-first hooks outperform component-first hooks across multiple comparable topics.

## Unknown / open questions

- Whether four posts/day remains optimal after audience growth changes the baseline.
- Which CTA produces the best follower conversion without reducing completion.
- Whether technical depth has an inverted-U effect on completion or sharing.
- Whether visual format or topic explains more variance in distribution.
- How much search traffic can be grown intentionally from problem-language titles and captions.
- Whether lifestyle content should remain at 10–15% or be reduced further.

## Next gate — MK1

MK1 can close only when:

1. A minimum usable sample of post-level data exists across multiple days and formats.
2. Metrics are normalized per post and by objective.
3. Topic, hook, format and CTA are represented as explicit experiment dimensions.
4. Duplicate/reused concepts can be detected from an editorial ledger.
5. At least one current hypothesis has survived repeated comparison rather than a single winning post.
6. Contradictory evidence is preserved instead of averaged away.

## Claim discipline

Current status permits statements such as:

> “Current Logan evidence favors diagnostic, symptom-first content.”

It does **not** permit:

> “Symptom-first content always performs better on TikTok.”
