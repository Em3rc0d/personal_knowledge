# Content Growth — MK0

## Purpose

Mine and frame the first evidence-backed content growth model using the Logan / `mi.logan.pe` TikTok account as the initial case.

## Inputs

- 7-day TikTok account metrics reviewed on 2026-09-11;
- recent post-level performance;
- surfaced search-query language;
- established working process of four daily posts;
- existing editorial rules around quality, non-repetition and experiment measurement.

## Outputs

- account-specific metric baseline;
- distribution-to-conversion bottleneck hypothesis;
- symptom-first editorial thesis;
- aggressive-but-honest hook contract;
- four-post daily experiment loop;
- no-repeat contract;
- CTA-by-objective system;
- semantic hashtag rule;
- `T+2h / T+24h / T+7d` measurement windows;
- GREEN / YELLOW / RED decision model;
- falsification and cadence gates.

## What MK0 does not claim

MK0 does not claim that:

- four posts/day is globally optimal;
- symptom-first always beats component-first;
- any single recent winning post proves causation;
- like rate is the primary success metric;
- Logan-specific observations transfer directly to other accounts or platforms.

## Promotion state

**Baseline knowledge is suitable for canonical storage.**

MK0 as a research phase remains active until enough longitudinal post-level evidence exists to normalize baselines and close the MK1 entry conditions described in `STATUS.md`.
